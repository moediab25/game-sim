Plate 819 mm wide (maze area 795 × 1200 mm), 20 × 20 cells of 39.75 × 60 mm, walls read from the SolidWorks drawing, 39 gates (orange = floor gate, blue = push gate, grey-blue = roof gate that cannot reach a resting ball). The four buttons are push left, push right, open floor and lift. A press works the gate in the ball's own cell. The CAD does not say which dot recess is which colour or which bottom hole is the exit, so the coloured dots and the exit were picked by a solver so that the maze can be won. Edit CODE, DOTS, EXIT and RIDDLES at the top of the script.
