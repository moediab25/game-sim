The Trick Wall

TER06. Steer the ball through green, red, yellow, blue, then out the exit.
How it works. You can't touch the ball. Each button secretly does push left, push right, open floor or lift, and the mapping reshuffles after every run. A press only works if the ball sits in a cell with the matching gate. Keys 1-4 or Q W E R. Orange = floor gate, blue = push gate, grey-blue = roof gate (can't reach a resting ball).
